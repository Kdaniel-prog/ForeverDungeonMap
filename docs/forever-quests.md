- Véletlenszerű helyek (egy futásban egy):
  1. Crypt by the fountain: from the entrance go south to the fountain in the spider courtyard, turn right to a crypt with a closed door. Open the door, a banshee comes out, and the crest hangs on the wall behind her.
  2. Tower to the right of Bjork's patrol area (the room with a staircase across from Bjork's courtyard): the crest lies on the floor below the stairs. A treasure chest can also spawn in this room.
  3. Behind Bjork's patrol, north-west of Bjork: on the back wall inside the small pavilion / temple building.
  4. Tower to the left of the spider courtyard near the start, just past Witherfang: climb the spiral stairs inside, and the crest is on the outside wall at the top.
